The Challenge

Solve a Real-World Problem

The 17 Sustainable Development Goals are a blueprint for a better world. Your challenge is to identify a pressing problem within one of the SDGs and build a tech-enabled solution in a 24-hour virtual sprint. The top 5 teams will advance to our in-person Grand Finale.

Hackathon Timeline

Registration Period

Now - December 16th, 2025

Submit your team and idea now. Registrations close on December 16th at 11:59 PM WAT.

The 24-Hour Virtual Sprint

December 17-18, 2025

All registered teams will build and code their solutions remotely.
Kickoff: December 17th at 12:00 PM WAT

Review & Finalist Selection

December 19-20, 2025

Our expert judges will review all submissions and select the top performers.
Top 5 Finalists will be notified by email on December 20th.

The Grand Finale: Pitch Day

December 20, 2025

The Top 5 teams will present a 10-minute pitch and demo in person.
Venue: University of Lagos
Time: 10:00 AM WAT

What to Expect

Prize Pool

₦500,000 will be shared across the top three teams, plus an exclusive incubation opportunity at TOS Hub to bring your idea to life.

Judging Criteria

Solutions will be judged on innovation, real-world impact on the SDGs, technical feasibility, and the quality of your final pitch.

The Finale Event

After the final pitches, join us for food, networking with builders and investors, and celebrate at our official photo banner.
